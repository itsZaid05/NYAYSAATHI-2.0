## Questions you may be asked

**“Does it currently retrieve actual law?”**

No approved authentic legal passages are loaded. The infrastructure and offline review gate exist, and fixture mechanics are tested. We withhold legal conclusions rather than fill that gap with fabricated authority. Actual law-backed acceptance is still blocked.

**“Why use it instead of a generic chatbot?”**

The value is the persistent preparation workflow: structured facts with original-text references, correction, chronology, issue-specific gaps, evidence organisation, editable documents, protected PDF and explicit handoff controls. A conversational answer alone does not provide that complete record. This does not make the AI an expert.

**“What does the readiness indicator mean?”**

Completeness and unresolved preparation gaps. It does not say the allegation is proven, the procedure is satisfied, the claim is strong or the user will win.

**“How is legal correctness checked?”**

Technical checks verify source/quote/reference integrity and a separate AI prompt can assess support. Actual source authenticity, rights, effective version and substantive applicability require independent review. AI agreement is not proof of legal correctness.

**“Is the data encrypted and anonymous?”**

The prototype has consent and access controls, private runtime permissions, redaction, protected downloads and deletion. It does not claim end-to-end encryption, encrypted SQLite by default or complete anonymity. Real confidential-data processing remains gated.

**“Where is the trained model or blockchain?”**

Neither a custom legal outcome model nor blockchain logging was implemented. Gemini is a general provider model; embeddings support retrieval, and audit records are ordinary database rows.

**“What if Gemini or the internet fails?”**

Show the actual availability/fallback notice. Local factual preparation can continue, but do not call it successful live AI. Use a rehearsed synthetic case without pretending a cached or recorded interaction is a live result.

**“Does the demonstration contact a real lawyer or government portal?”**

No. It shares a snapshot with the explicitly labelled demo recipient and records receipt. Portal links are guidance starting points, not a completed submission or professional engagement.
